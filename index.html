SANCTUARY HOW-TO-PLAY 💖

👑 1. FEED & EVOLVE THE SLIME QUEEN

In the center sits the lovely Slime Queen, starting at Level 1. Launch incoming slimes toward her! When slimes in the cluster merge and reach the Queen's size, touching the Queen feeds her, leveling her up (Level 1 → 11)! Keep the garden happy and within the starlight ring.

✨ 2. SUN & MOON SLIMES

Slimes come in two lovely types:

  • ✨ SUN (Gold) & 🌙 MOON (Starlight): Same types gently repel; opposite types attract across space!
  • HARMONIC FUSION: Merging two slimes of the same tier with OPPOSITE types (Sun + Moon) unleashes rainbow confetti, pulling nearby slimes toward the center with 2.5× score bonus!
  • SWEET FUSION: Merging two slimes of the SAME type yields standard score.

🌈 3. PAIR BLOOM

Merge to charge. Press Space or tap PAIR BLOOM to bring every matching pair together with confetti! Sun or Moon: both work. Each existing pair grows one level; the Queen, unpaired slimes and level 11 stay put. No pairs? Keep your charge.

🌸 4. STARLIGHT GARDEN PERIMETER

If your slimes push past the outer glowing floral ring, Sanctuary Harmony decreases. Merge slimes to keep the garden cozy and calm.

Mouse or drag to aim; release on the field to launch. Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ to aim, Enter or ↓ to launch, Space for Pair Bloom, M for sound, R to restart. Sun has a gold sun badge; Moon has a blue crescent badge. Cursor-following eyes can be switched off in Settings.
