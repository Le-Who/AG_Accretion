NUCLEUS ACCRETION PROTOCOLS

1. OBJECTIVE: NUCLEUS EVOLUTION

In the center sits the Zero-Point Nucleus, starting at Tier 1. Launch incoming cores inward toward the nucleus. When surrounding cores in the cluster merge and reach the same size as the Nucleus, fusing into the central core levels up the Nucleus (Tier 1 → 11)! Keep the expanding mass within the circular perimeter.

2. MAGNETIC POLARITY & RESONANCE

Cores carry magnetic dipole charges:

  • +ALPHA (Amber) & -BETA (Cyan): Like charges repel; opposite charges attract across distance!
  • RESONANT FUSION: Merging two cores of the same tier with OPPOSITE polarities (+ and -) creates an implosive shockwave that pulls nearby matter inward toward the nucleus, yielding 2.5× energy.
  • FORCED FUSION: Merging two cores of the same tier with SAME polarity (+/+ or -/-) yields standard score.

3. SINGULARITY FLUX PULSE

Press Space or tap FLUX PULSE when charged to invert all polarities on the board (+ becomes -, - becomes +), triggering a cascading magnetic collapse toward the central core!

4. CONTAINMENT BOUNDARY

If accreted matter pushes past the outer dashed perimeter ring, Containment Integrity drops. Fuse or shift cores inward before a total breach occurs.
