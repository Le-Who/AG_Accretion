# Task entrypoint

For code, test, UI, tooling, or asset changes, read [ownership and conventions](CODING_STANDARDS.md#code-ownership-and-conventions), follow every applicable [task route](CODING_STANDARDS.md#task-contracts) before editing, and complete [runtime validation](CODING_STANDARDS.md#runtime-validation).

For documentation-only changes, complete [documentation validation](CODING_STANDARDS.md#documentation-validation). Descriptions of gameplay, presentation, tuning, or reproducibility also follow the applicable task routes to resolve live behavior.
